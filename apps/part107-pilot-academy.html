PART 107 OPERATING RULES & AIRSPACE MATRIX

Max Groundspeed & Altitude

87 knots (100 mph) maximum groundspeed. 400 feet AGL maximum altitude (or within 400 feet of a structure if higher).

Weather Minimums (107.51)

Minimum 3 statute miles (3 SM) flight visibility from control station. Cloud clearance: 500 feet below and 2,000 feet horizontally.

Night Operations (107.29)

Permitted with updated training/testing and anti-collision lighting visible for at least 3 statute miles with flash rate sufficient to avoid collision.

Class B, C, D & Surface E

Requires prior ATC authorization (via LAANC or DroneZone). Class G airspace requires no prior ATC authorization.

Accident Reporting (107.9)

Report to FAA within 10 calendar days if serious injury, loss of consciousness, or property damage (other than the sUAS) exceeds $500.

Operations Over People (Subpart D)

Category 1: ≤ 0.55 lbs including everything onboard and no exposed rotating parts that would lacerate human skin.
